Types of Industrial Cranes

A quick reference to common industrial crane types and what each is generally used for. KBS's own core focus is the mobile gantry crane — highlighted below.

KBS Core Focus

Rubber-Tired Gantry Crane (RTG)

A mobile gantry crane mounted on rubber tires, able to move under its own power across a fixed span.

Commonly used forContainer terminals and yards — stacking, sorting, and moving shipping containers between vessels, trucks, and rail.
Road-Mobile

Mobile Crane

Mounted on a wheeled vehicle, allowing it to be driven directly to a site without special transport.

Commonly used forShort-term lifts, road and bridge work, and equipment installation — jobs needing a crane driven in and out quickly.
Indoor / Repetitive Lifts

Overhead Bridge Crane

Travels along elevated rails inside a building, well suited to repetitive lifting tasks along a fixed indoor span.

Commonly used forFactories, workshops, and warehouses — moving machinery, materials, or finished goods along a set path.
Workstation-Level

Jib Crane

A smaller crane, often wall- or column-mounted, with a rotating arm for focused, localized lifting.

Commonly used forWorkshop stations, loading bays, and maintenance areas where a full crane isn't needed.
Fixed Rail

Gantry Crane

A crane mounted on a gantry frame running on fixed rails, spanning a work area between two supporting legs.

Commonly used forShipyards, rail yards, and outdoor storage areas needing repeated lifts across a fixed path.
Fixed Rail / Building-Supported

Semi-Gantry Crane

Similar to a gantry crane, but one side runs on an elevated rail while the other travels along a lower rail or the building structure itself.

Commonly used forFacilities where one side of the crane's path can use an existing runway or building support.
Industrial Process

Process Crane

Engineered for repetitive, precise lifting within a specific industrial process, often timed to work alongside a production line.

Commonly used forManufacturing plants where material needs to move through a set sequence of process steps.
Heavy Duty / High Heat

Steel Mill Crane

A heavy-duty crane built for the extreme heat, load, and duty-cycle demands found in steel production environments.

Commonly used forHandling ladles, coils, slabs, and scrap in steel mills and foundries.
Automated

Automated Production Crane

Integrated with automated controls to move materials through a production line with minimal manual operation.

Commonly used forHigh-volume manufacturing and warehouse environments running automated material flow.
Single-Rail Path

Monorail Crane

A crane or hoist that travels along a single overhead rail, typically following one fixed, repeated path.

Commonly used forAssembly lines and workshops moving loads along one consistent route.
General industry reference — not a list of KBS's fleet or available equipment
